Mushroom Radio is a box—a radio / broadcasting platform, located at The Royal Academy of Arts in The Hague and run by its students.

The intention is to find or create a space for people to shape their voices together, share discoveries, works, music, to speak and listen each other, to react together.

It has been passed from one hands into the others and will continue to do so and change. Initiated by Esther Vane, Benjamin Earl and Jack Bardwell in 2019, currently managed by Klaudia Orczykowska, Andre Kahveciyan and Vlad Omelinaneko.
